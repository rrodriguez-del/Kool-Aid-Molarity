Need a calculation hint?

First divide mL by 1,000 to get liters. Multiply molarity by liters to get moles. Multiply moles by 40 g/mol to get grams.

About this model & how to use it

Use 40 g/mol for the powder, as assigned in the original lab. Real drink mix contains several substances and has no single molar mass. All powder is assumed to dissolve; color illustrates relative concentration rather than measured absorbance.

For each part: predict the mass, tare the balance, set mass and final solution volume, mix, and record. Then compare the four results and complete Explain. This virtual version replaces tasting with color and numerical evidence.

Five useful chemistry words
Solute
The substance dissolved: the drink powder.
Solvent
The dissolving substance: water.
Solution
A homogeneous mixture of solute and solvent.
Meniscus
The curved surface of a liquid. Read its bottom at eye level.
Molarity (M)
Moles of solute per liter of final solution.
